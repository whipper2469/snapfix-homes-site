FAQ

Questions, answered

What can SnapFix diagnose?

Plumbing leaks and clogs, wall and ceiling damage, appliance error codes, outlets and switches, pests, paint and surface damage, fixtures, and more. If you can photograph it, start there.

How accurate is it?

Every diagnosis carries a confidence score, so you always know how certain the answer is. Add up to three angles for a better read, and use follow-up chat to ask about your specific situation.

Does this replace a professional?

No — and it will tell you so. For anything complex, dangerous or code-regulated, like major electrical or gas work, it points you to a licensed pro rather than talking you through it.

What happens to my photos?

They generate your diagnosis and are stored with your account so you can revisit past repairs. They are never sold. See the Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone on iOS 17 or later. An Android version is in the works.

How do I cancel?

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SnapFix → Cancel. Pro access runs to the end of the billing period.
